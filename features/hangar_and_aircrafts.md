# 🛸 Hangar & Aircraft Specs

Galaxy Fighter includes 5 distinct playable fighters with custom handling characteristics:

1. **Red Falcon (Plane 1 Red)**:
   - All-round balanced tactical fighter.
   - Agile vertical banking and rapid muzzle recovery.
2. **Blue Stealth (Plane 1 Blue)**:
   - High-speed aerodynamic interceptor.
   - Boosted base speed (+10%).
3. **Thunder Gold (Plane 1 Yellow)**:
   - Heavy plasma conduits with wider torpedo blast radius.
4. **Viper (Plane 2 Green)**:
   - Advanced prototype with enhanced magnet attraction radius.
5. **Cosmo Cruiser (Plane 3 Blue)**:
   - Heavy galactic battleship with +1 bonus missile magazine capacity.
