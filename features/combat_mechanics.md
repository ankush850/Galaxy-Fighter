# ⚔️ Combat Mechanics & Weapon Systems

### Tactical 6-Round Magazine
- The aircraft primary weapon uses a 6-torpedo magazine clip.
- **Auto-Reload**: When the 6th bullet is spent, an automated 1.0s tactical reload sequence engages with on-screen gauge and sound feedback.
- **Manual Reload**: Players can manually initiate reload anytime using <kbd>R</kbd>, <kbd>K</kbd>, or the on-screen <kbd>○</kbd> circle button.

### Combo Multiplier System
- Eliminating multiple UFOs in rapid succession (within 2.8s) builds a **Combo Multiplier** (`x2`, `x3`, `x4`, `x5` Score multiplier).
- Taking damage or allowing the timer to expire resets the combo streak to zero.

### Boss Multi-Phase Mechanics
- **Phase 1 (100% - 65% HP)**: Precision targeted single fireballs.
- **Phase 2 (65% - 30% HP)**: 3-way spread fireballs + vertical evasive sweeps.
- **Phase 3 (30% - 0% HP)**: Enrage Mode with glowing red barrier, 5-way spread barrage, and drone escorts.
